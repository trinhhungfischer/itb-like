# Encounter Template Creation

**Description:** Author the Encounter Generator templates for different terrain themes and encounter types to ensure balanced compositions.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Templates respect the composition rules (e.g. Battle = 3-5 enemies, min 1 Approach + 1 Artillery/Zone).
2. Terrain themes (Ruins, Inferno, Badlands, Hive) spawn enemies that synergize with the environment (e.g., Lobbers in Inferno, Sentinels in Badlands).
3. The encounter generator correctly samples the T1/T2/T3 ratios based on the current Difficulty Tier.
4. Boss encounters correctly spawn 1 Boss and 2-4 minions.

**Story Type:** Content
**Story Points:** 3
**Dependencies:** Tier Stat Scaling, Overseer Aura System, Boss Alternating AI
